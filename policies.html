Club Policies

Velvet Cue Lounge, Qurum, Muscat · Last updated: October 2026
Hours & rates Bookings Payment Cancellation House rules Privacy Contact

1. Opening hours and rates

  • We are open daily from 12:00 PM to 5:00 AM.
  • Off-peak rate: 12:00 PM to 8:00 PM. Peak rate: 8:00 PM to 5:00 AM.
  • Current prices are shown at the counter and on the booking page. All prices are in Omani Rials (OMR) and may change without notice.

2. Bookings

  • Walk-in customers are served at the counter, subject to availability.
  • An online booking is confirmed only after our staff approve it. A submitted request is not a confirmed booking.
  • For advance bookings, please arrive between 10 minutes before and 5 minutes after your booked time. If you do not arrive in this window, the booking is cancelled automatically and the table is released.
  • Playing time starts when our staff start the table timer.
  • Extra time can be added by staff on request, if the table is not booked by someone else.
  • During tournaments or private events, some or all tables may not be available for booking.

3. Payment

  • Payment is made at the counter, by cash or card, at the end of your session.
  • Charges are based on the time played and any canteen items ordered for your table.
  • Discounts and offers are at the discretion of the management.

4. Cancellation

  • No advance payment is taken for online bookings, so there is no cancellation fee.
  • If you cannot come, please let us know on WhatsApp so we can release the table for other players.
  • The management may cancel or move a booking if a table is unavailable, for example for maintenance.

5. House rules

  • Please handle cues, balls, tables and consoles with care. Damage caused by misuse may be charged.
  • Please respect other players and keep noise at a reasonable level.
  • The management may refuse service to anyone who does not follow these rules.

6. Privacy

When you book online, we collect your name and mobile number, and the details of your booking.

  • We use this information only to manage your booking and to contact you about it.
  • We do not sell or share your information with anyone for marketing.
  • Booking records are stored securely and kept for the club's business and accounting records.
  • If you confirm on WhatsApp, the message is sent by you through WhatsApp and is also covered by WhatsApp's own privacy terms.
  • To ask for your details to be corrected or deleted, contact us using the details below.
